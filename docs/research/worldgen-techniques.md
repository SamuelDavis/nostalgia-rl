# Established worldgen techniques

A step back from phase 1: which accepted methods fit our constraints, which are infinite (chunked, bounded-neighbourhood), deterministic across engines, and cheap per chunk. Researched 2026-10-16.

## Verdict

The pipeline shape is the standard one: noise fields, then biome classification, then rivers following drainage, then settlements, then least-cost routes. The ad hoc parts are the forest trails' random walks and the separate route types. A single cost field with route reuse would replace both, and it is better grounded.

## Applicable

| Technique                                                                                                                                                           | What it gives us                                                                                                                                                      | Fit                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Anisotropic least-cost roads ([Galin et al. 2010](https://perso.liris.cnrs.fr/egalin/Articles/2010-roads.pdf))                                                      | Routes over a cost field: slope, rivers, forests, curvature. Roads carved along the path.                                                                             | Already what `routing.ts` does, with fewer cost terms. Add vegetation, water and curvature terms, and a larger neighbourhood mask to avoid grid-straight lines.                |
| Proximity-graph networks ([Galin et al. 2011](https://perso.liris.cnrs.fr/eric.guerin/download/pg2011.pdf); [RNG roads](https://toakraka.nfshost.com/000173.xhtml)) | Which settlements connect: the relative neighbourhood or Gabriel graph under a terrain-aware metric gives natural networks without redundant crossings.               | Local test over neighbouring camps, so it is chunk-safe. Would replace the random `ROAD_LINKS`.                                                                                |
| Active walker trails ([Helbing et al. 1997](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/cond-mat/9806097); [human trail systems](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/9805158))     | Walkers prefer ground already walked, so trails merge into networks instead of crossing. Pedestrians form minimal detour systems; ants form dendritic ones.           | Approximate with A\* and a cost discount on existing trail tiles, routing a macro cell's trails in a fixed order. This fixes crossing trails and gives branching from merging. |
| Spline-shaped noise ([Minecraft 1.18](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Terrain); [overview](https://dawnosaur.substack.com/p/how-minecraft-generates-worlds-you))           | Continentalness, erosion and peaks-and-valleys noises remapped through splines, which allow deliberate steep changes (cliffs, plateaus) that smooth noise can't make. | Pure per-point functions, so chunk-safe. A tunable replacement for hand formulas in `fields.ts`, if terrain shape needs work.                                                  |

## Known but not a fit now

| Technique                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Why not now                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Drainage graphs ([Red Blob polygon maps](https://www.redblobgames.com/maps/polygon-map-generation/), [mapgen4](https://www.redblobgames.com/maps/mapgen4/)) and Priority-Flood ([Barnes et al. 2014](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1511.04463), O(N log N)) | They give tributaries that merge into dendritic rivers, but flow is global. On an infinite world it needs regions with halos. Worth it only if rivers become a playtest complaint. |
| Erosion simulation (hydraulic, stream power)                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Best-looking terrain, but global, iterative and costly. It conflicts with infinite, per-chunk, bit-identical generation.                                                           |
